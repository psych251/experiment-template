# Original paper

Put the paper you are replicating here, along with any supplementary materials and anything the
original authors sent you (materials, data, analysis code). The replication report, the course
staff, and any agent reviewing your project all look here first for what the original study did.

**This repository is public.** Many papers cannot legally be redistributed. If the paper is not
open access, check its licence before committing the PDF; if you cannot share it, keep the
citation and DOI in the report and a link here instead. Materials the authors sent you privately
should not be committed without their permission.
